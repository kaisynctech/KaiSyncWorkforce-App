Modules

What you can run on KaiSync

Six clear groups. Turn them on per company — no clutter, no forced suite.

Workforce

The people spine of the product — who is on shift, who is on leave, and how pay gets calculated.

  • Employees, work teams, attendance, team punch
  • Leave, time templates, scheduling
  • Payroll runs, payslips, statutory-ready deductions

Operations

How work gets sold and delivered in the field — with a clear trail for clients and contractors.

  • Clients, projects, jobs, and job media
  • Contractors and compliance packs
  • Incidents with assignment and follow-up

Money

Finance that sits next to operations instead of in a separate spreadsheet world.

  • Invoices, VAT, cashflow views
  • Approvals and contractor payouts

Supply & Assets

Materials and equipment that keep jobs moving.

  • Suppliers and inventory
  • Company assets and allocations

Properties

Built for managing agents and multi-site operators.

  • Properties and site context
  • Residents linked to operational work

Insights & portals

Visibility for leadership — and scoped access for people outside HR.

  • Reports and My PA
  • Client portal and contractor portal
  • Messaging and notifications
