Modules

Everything KaiSync can run for your company.

Six module groups. Enable what you need. Same structure your teams see in the product sidebar.

Workforce Operations Money Supply & Assets Properties Insights & portals

01 · Workforce

People, time, leave, and payroll in one spine.

Workforce is the operational core for HR and supervisors. Every punch, leave decision, and payslip hangs off the same employee record — so month-end stops being a reconciliation project.

  • Employees & work teams — org structure, access levels, and team grouping
  • Attendance & team punch — GPS-backed clock events and supervisor punch flows
  • Leave & time templates — balances, approvals, and shift patterns that stay payroll-ready
  • Scheduling — who should be where, when
  • Payroll — salary/hourly runs, deductions, payslip release

02 · Operations

Sell work, assign the field, prove delivery.

Operations connects commercial work to site execution. Clients and projects feed jobs; contractors and employees execute; incidents and compliance stay on the same trail instead of in email threads.

  • Clients & projects — commercial context and progress
  • Jobs — field work, media, checklists, and status
  • Contractors — assignment plus scoped contractor portal access
  • Incidents — report, assign, investigate, close
  • Compliance packs — documents and requirements tied to delivery

03 · Money

Finance that can see completed work.

Money sits next to operations so invoices and approvals aren’t guessing. Finance users get cashflow and approval tools without losing the link to jobs and contractor work.

  • Finance overview — operationally aware money views
  • Invoices & VAT — billing that matches how you deliver
  • Approvals — controlled release with an audit trail
  • Contractor payouts — visibility into what is owed and queued

04 · Supply & Assets

Stock and equipment that keep jobs moving.

Supply & Assets tracks what you buy, store, and allocate. Ops teams see shortages before a crew arrives empty-handed; finance sees usage that actually happened.

  • Suppliers — vendor records tied to procurement
  • Inventory — stock levels, movements, and alerts
  • Assets — company equipment allocated across sites

05 · Properties

Sites and residents beside the work.

Built for managing agents and multi-site operators. Brief contractors against real properties and residents — not a detached spreadsheet of addresses.

  • Properties — sites and portfolio context
  • Residents — people linked to units and service history
  • Operational handovers — jobs and incidents with place context

06 · Insights & portals

Signal for leadership. Scoped access for partners.

Insights gives internal teams reporting and personal follow-through. Portals give clients and contractors a secure lane into only what they should see — without handing out full HR access.

  • Reports — operational and executive exports leadership can trust
  • My PA — personal tasks and reminders
  • Messaging & notifications — in-product communication
  • Client portal — scoped project and communication access
  • Contractor portal — jobs, site visits, and documents for partners
