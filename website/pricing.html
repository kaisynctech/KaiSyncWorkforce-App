What counts as a portal contractor?

Only contractors marked as portal users. Directory contacts without portal access do not use a seat.

Do inactive employees or properties bill?

No. Only active employees and active properties count. Deactivate a record to stop billing for it.

Where do we see what we owe?

In the app under Settings → Billing — live monthly amount with a full breakdown.
