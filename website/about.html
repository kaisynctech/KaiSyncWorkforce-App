About

Built for real operations

KaiSync Workforce is a multi-tenant web platform from KaiSync Tech — modular, auditable, and installable as a PWA.

We help facility operators, field services, managing agents, and multi-site employers replace scattered spreadsheets and chat threads with one secure system.

HR, employees, clients, and contractors each get the right experience — company-scoped data, role-based access, and modules you enable when you need them.

The product is web-first. Teams sign in at kaisyncworkforce.com and can install KaiSync on any device without a separate native package.
