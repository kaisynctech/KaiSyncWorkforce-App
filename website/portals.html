Portals

Three experiences. One platform.

Employees run their day. Clients see project truth. Contractors execute in the field — each with the right access, not a shared inbox.

Employee Client Contractor

Employee portal

Workforce self-service for people who do the work.

The employee experience is a full web workplace — not a thin noticeboard. Field and office staff sign in with company + employee code, or email and password / OTP, then get only the modules their company enables.

How they sign in: company code + employee login code, or email authentication. Installable as a PWA on phone or desktop.

  • Clock in / out — optional job, notes, branch geofence; offline queue when signal drops
  • My Jobs & My Shifts — see assigned work and scheduled shifts
  • Leave & attendance — request leave, track status, review punch history
  • Incidents — report and follow open incidents
  • Payslips & documents — when payroll/docs modules are on
  • My PA, messages & notifications — personal follow-through without WhatsApp chaos
  • Profile — keep personal and banking details current for HR

Client portal

Project visibility without giving clients your HR login.

Clients get a scoped lane into the work you are delivering for them. Your project manager issues company + client codes — no password setup, no full platform seat.

How they sign in: company code + client code only. Built for quick access on any browser.

  • Projects list — status, progress, commercial totals, last update
  • Project detail — quotation lines, payment history, visit/close dates
  • Activity & photos — progress updates and site media they can trust
  • Messaging — talk to the project team with an unread inbox
  • Documents — view shared files; upload docs or add links on a project
  • Invoices — see invoice list and outstanding balance

Clients do not get online checkout or admin tools — the portal is for clarity and collaboration, while your team keeps control of finance and delivery.

Contractor portal

Field partners execute, prove, and get paid — in their own lane.

Contractors work from a dedicated portal scoped to their firm. They sign in with company + contractor codes and only see jobs and commercial items assigned to them.

How they sign in: company code + contractor code. Designed for site phones as much as office desktops.

  • Jobs dashboard — open jobs, active site visit, outstanding payouts at a glance
  • Site GPS sign-in / out — visit history tied to the job
  • Before / after photos — proof of work on the record
  • Job messaging & incidents — communicate and escalate without leaving the job
  • Quotes — draft lines, upload quote files, submit and track approval
  • Invoices & payouts — submit for HR review; track or resubmit payout status
  • Banking & compliance — submit banking details and compliance pack documents for company approval
  • Trading profile — keep registration, tax/VAT, and contact details current
